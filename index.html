ABOUT PALATEOPS

Good planning, with receipts.

PalateOps turns a cafeteria brief into a deterministic five-day menu, checks hard constraints, and totals procurement quantities. Live Qloo mode is a server-side integration and requires an authorized event credential. Sample signals are synthetic and clearly labeled.

Allergen metadata is a planning screen only. Always verify the final recipe, supplier labels and cross-contact controls with your kitchen team.
